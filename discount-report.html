Data coverage & exclusions

Paid order records in USD. Cleffo test payments and abandoned checkouts are excluded. Tagada orders are excluded from all totals. Other historical processor orders are included as recorded paid; their test status and detailed discounts, shipping, and tax were not saved. Product sales exclude shipping and tax. Figures are before refunds, processor fees, and product costs. Date filters use New York time.
