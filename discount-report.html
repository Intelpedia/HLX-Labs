Sales overview

Gross revenue is product sales before discounts. Net revenue* is gross less recorded discounts, before refunds. Both exclude shipping and tax. Refunds, fees, and product costs are not recorded here; net revenue is not profit.

Costs & estimated profit

Estimates apply the currently supplied purchase prices and cost rules to the selected order history; these are not historical cost snapshots. Net product revenue less the specified costs below gives estimated profit. Refunds and other overhead are not included. Customer shipping charges are excluded from product revenue; no shipping expense below the free-shipping threshold has been supplied.
