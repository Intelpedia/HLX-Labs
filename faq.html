Frequently Asked Questions

What are HLX Labs products intended for?

Products and information on this website are offered for laboratory research use only. They are not intended for use in or on humans or animals, or for diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention of any condition.

Where can I find a Certificate of Analysis?

Visit the COA Library. It automatically displays the COA image associated with each product in the catalog.

Do I need an account to place an order?

No. The current storefront is designed to support guest checkout. Accounts can be offered as an optional way to access features such as order history once production authentication is connected.

What payment methods are accepted?

Accepted payment methods will be shown at checkout once the production payment processor is connected.

When is my order confirmed?

An order should be treated as confirmed only after the production payment processor reports a successful payment and an order confirmation is issued.

How will I receive my order confirmation?

The site includes an order-confirmation page. Transactional email confirmation can also be connected when the production email and payment services are configured.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Contact HLX Labs as soon as possible. Whether an order can be changed or cancelled depends on its fulfillment status and the final Shipping & Returns Policy.

How are shipping and returns handled?

Please review the Shipping & Returns Policy. Processing times, carriers, rates, and final return rules should be updated before launch.

How can I contact HLX Labs?

Use the Contact page. Final support email, phone, mailing address, and business hours can be added when available.
